Twenty-nine more networks, and a download that no longer stops behind your back.

• New networks: Oslo, Trondheim, Long Beach, Vitoria-Gasteiz, Uničov, Ravne na Koroškem, and twenty-three North American networks, among them Los Angeles, Milwaukee, Cincinnati, Salt Lake City, Nashville and San Antonio. Nine networks that no longer publish their stations have been withdrawn.
• If the network you chose has been withdrawn, the application now says so and offers another city, instead of asking which city you want as on a first installation. Its downloaded data stays listed, so you can delete it when you choose.
• Fixed: leaving the Storage screen while a city was downloading stopped the download without a word. It now carries on, and the map says it is downloading.
• Fixed: when a download was cut by a lost connection, nothing happened once the connection came back. The Storage screen now tells you, and "Download" picks up where it stopped.
• Fixed: in Limoges, every Pony station was shown out of service while bikes could be taken from them.
• Fixed: GraouLib', in Metz, showed no station since the network moved its data.
