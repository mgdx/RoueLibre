Six cities more, and one of them was being refused over a handbike.

• Wrocław, Bogotá, Offenburg, Sibiu, Buzău and Slobozia join the catalogue. Bogotá is the first city served in South America.
• They had all been turned away by a rule that read anything GBFS cannot name — a handbike, a tricycle, a bike trailer — as a motor vehicle. What has no name of its own is now judged on whether it is pedalled.
• A city named by the catalogue that this version cannot serve is shown greyed out, saying so, instead of being chosen and leaving every screen as though no city had been picked.
