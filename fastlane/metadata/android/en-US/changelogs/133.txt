Ten cities' data did not cover the whole city, and it has been made again.

• Fixed: Washington's map, journey planner and address search stopped at the edge of the District of Columbia, and nine other cities had a hole of the same kind. The area each city's data is cut out of was only checked at twenty-five points, so a whole region could be missed; it is now checked every kilometre. Brive-la-Gaillarde, Niort, Épinal, Toulouse, Washington, Vienna's regional network, Zagreb, the Grand Est's Vélo Fluo, Belgium's Blue-bike and Zurich have been published again: open Storage, check for updates, and download the city again to get all of it.
• Fixed: a city's map did not appear properly after it had been downloaded — the old one was still being drawn. The new map now appears straight away, without closing and reopening the application. The map also keeps the view you left it at only when you come back to the same city.
• The what's new screen shows the three most recent versions instead of every version since the last one you saw, and each note now says which version it belongs to. It also appears in all thirty-one languages: outside English and French there were no notes at all, and the screen simply did not open.
• Fixed: outside English and French, the setting for the bikes left outside the stations described only half of what it does. It also shows how charged the bikes standing at a station are, and it now says so in every language.
• Fixed: the whole catalogue was downloaded every time the list of cities was opened, for nothing. It is now downloaded only when it has changed — less mobile data and Wi-Fi used.
