Journeys work again where bikes are left without a dock, and the station list works like the map.

• Munich, Düsseldorf, Freiburg and the other networks where bikes go back to a zone or beside a full station: journeys end there again, and free docks are no longer shown where they mean nothing. The same goes for Auch, Niort and the other Ecovelo networks.
• A new setting under your city, "Full stations take bikes back", can be set to Automatic, Always or Never.
• The station list has the map's buttons and filters, and your favourites at its top.
• The kind of bike chosen on the map is kept from one launch to the next, and a journey shows which bike you ride.
• Picking a point on the map opens where the map was.
• New network: Mainz. Pau is back.
• Fixed: with a theme other than the system's, the application could open on an empty screen.
• Fixed: "System" units followed the application's language rather than your phone's region.
